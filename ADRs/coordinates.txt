We are going to use same as WebGPU/WebGL axises in coordinate system:
https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Games/Techniques/3D_on_the_web/Basic_theory/mdn-games-3d-coordinate-system.png

It's just to follow industry standards. Otherwise all already implemented matrix functions like aimAt works
absolutely unpredictably and are the potential source of the cancer for the author of the code.

The other option was to place elements on x, y:
          ↑
          |
          |
x -------------------→
          |
          |
          |
          y
and rotate camera by x axis by around 4 degrees, so Z axis will be the "height" of objects.
Unfortunately that brings tons of problems with matricies and camera angle rotations.

The downside of our chosen approach that we will have to remember Z axis is not like Y axis
in common carthesian space, it's reversed like in all computes sience graphics.
